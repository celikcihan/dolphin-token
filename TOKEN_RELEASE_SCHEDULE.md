\# Vesting Schedule



A total of \*\*200,000,000 DOLPHIN\*\* is locked in the public vesting contract.



Vesting parameters:



\- Cliff Period: 12 months

\- Vesting Duration: 36 months

\- Unlock Frequency: Monthly

\- Unlock Method: Linear Vesting



No vested tokens can be released before the cliff period expires.



The vesting contract is publicly verifiable on BaseScan.

\# Burn Policy



A total of \*\*100,000,000 DOLPHIN\*\* has been permanently burned through on-chain transactions.



Burned tokens are sent to an unrecoverable burn address and are permanently removed from circulation.



No additional minting is possible.
